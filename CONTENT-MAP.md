## Answer checking

Each text has 20 activities, for a total of 200. Choice and True / False answers are compared with their keys. Gap answers accept one word from the text, ignoring capitalization, surrounding whitespace and final punctuation. Free predictions are not automatically graded. Ordering tasks award one point only for the complete correct sequence.

The five ordering tasks cover the opening, later episodes, the ending and an overall story plan. Some key events reappear in the overall plan to move from individual details to the structure of the whole story.
